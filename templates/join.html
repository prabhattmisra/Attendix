How it works

  1. Start the front camera and capture a new selfie.
  2. Enter the active code shared by your teacher.
  3. Allow location access if your teacher configured a classroom location.
  4. Submit. Attend75 checks the session and prevents duplicate attendance.

Important: camera capture alone cannot prove liveness or prevent a student from showing a photo/video to the camera. For stronger anti-proxy checks, pair this with a rotating QR code, classroom location verification and authorised manual review or a properly tested liveness/face-verification service.
